Avertum Private Markets Position Review

When an unlisted holding becomes unclear, you need verifiable facts – not more promises.

Avertum helps private investors separate verified facts from claims and information gaps in illiquid or difficult-to-assess unlisted holdings.

We do not sell investments, broker shares or receive commissions from companies, platforms or intermediaries. We deliver an independent decision basis – without guarantees of liquidity, recovery or financial outcome.

From uncertainty to structured clarity

A defined mandate, a verifiable method and a deliverable that can inform your next decision or be handed to an external specialist.

01 · MATERIALS

Mandate and documents

We define the question and review agreements, payment records, company communications and other relevant documents.

02 · VERIFICATION

Facts and discrepancies

We compare the information against lawfully accessible corporate, ownership and market sources and identify material information gaps.

03 · DELIVERY

Position and next steps

You receive a structured report, source index and map of possible next steps – without a buy, sell or hold recommendation.

Clear packages. Fixed scope. No hidden fees.

Each engagement starts with a complimentary 20-minute fit assessment. Only matters that match our mandate are accepted.

Is this the right service for you?

Strong mandate fit

  • An unlisted or illiquid holding with material financial exposure
  • A delayed exit, listing or other promised liquidity event
  • Incomplete, contradictory or mainly verbal information
  • Uncertainty regarding ownership, terms, SPVs or transfer restrictions
  • Access to agreements, payment records and communications

Not the right service

  • You require a guarantee of recovery, value or sale
  • You want personal buy, sell or hold advice
  • You want Avertum to broker shares or locate buyers
  • You request unauthorised access, harassment or deceptive methods
  • You lack a legitimate connection to the holding or subject
